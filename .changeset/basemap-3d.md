Basemap & 3D: globe projection (`<Map projection="globe">`), 3D terrain (`<Terrain>`, defaulting to a free elevation tileset, with optional sky), raster tile layers (`<RasterLayer>` for XYZ/WMS), and PMTiles support (`registerPmtilesProtocol()`, auto-registered by `<Map>` on a `pmtiles://` style). All new props/types carry full JSDoc.
